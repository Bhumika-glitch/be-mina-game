Be Mina

Every slide on the screen is blue, and everyone in the room says so. You are Mina, one of two planted voices in a colour study like Moscovici’s 1969 experiment, and your job is to call the blue slide green. For five days, decide how Mina behaves, and watch what the room says out loud and what it privately shifts toward.
